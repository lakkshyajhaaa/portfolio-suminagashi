Tunnet is an innovative music discovery platform that allows you to find customizable songs based on your specific musical preferences. 

Let's suppose you like a specific track, **Song A**, but you want it to have a heavier guitar presence. With Tunnet, you can edit the guitar parameter to be more prominent, and the platform will search for and suggest songs that are closest to **Song A**—but with the exact modifications you requested, like more guitar. 

*(This project is currently under development.)*
