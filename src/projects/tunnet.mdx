## Overview

Mainstream music streaming platforms rely heavily on collaborative filtering: algorithms recommend songs because "users who listened to Track X also listened to Track Y." While effective for popular listening habits, this creates acoustic echo chambers and provides zero user agency over musical composition.

**Tunnet** explores an alternative paradigm: **parametric music discovery**. If a user loves a song's chord structure but wants a rendition with heavier guitar presence, lower tempo, or warmer acoustic instrumentation, Tunnet allows them to tune those parameters directly, searching musical feature space to surface songs that match their exact acoustic preferences.

---

## 01. The Problem

1. **The "Black-Box" Recommendation Trap:** Listeners cannot communicate granular musical nuances to streaming algorithms. You cannot tell an algorithm: *"Find me songs that sound like this indie-rock track, but with stronger basslines and zero synthesizers."*
2. **Coarse Genre Categorization:** Traditional tags (e.g., "Rock", "Electronic") obscure the multidimensional continuous spectrum of musical attributes.
3. **Passive Consumption vs. Active Exploration:** Most music discovery interfaces are passive carousels that don't invite creative listener experimentation.

### Who Experiences This Problem?
Music enthusiasts, producers, sound designers, and curious listeners looking for specific sonic characteristics across unfamiliar artists and genres.

## 03. System Architecture

```text
[Seed Track Selection]
   │ (Extract baseline acoustic vector)
   ▼
[Parametric Modifier Interface]
   │ User adjusts: Guitar Weight, Bass Presence, Tempo, Energy
   ▼
[Dynamic Vector Calculation]
   │ v_target = v_seed + Δ_user
   ▼
[Feature Distance Engine]
   │ Computes cosine / Euclidean distance in normalized feature space
   ▼
[Ranked Acoustic Match Candidates]
   │ Real-time preview snippet playback
   ▼
[Curated User Playlist Generation]
```

## 04. Engineering Challenges & Trade-offs

### Challenge 1: Multi-Parametric Interface Usability
- **Constraint:** Exposing 10+ acoustic variables simultaneously overwhelms non-technical listeners and creates visual clutter.
- **Alternative Considered:** Hidden advanced settings dropdowns. This made the core feature invisible to casual users.
- **Selected Approach:** Designed a radial coordinate system combined with three primary macro-sliders (Acoustic Texture, Harmonic Density, Rhythmic Drive) that expand into granular stem adjustments on demand.

### Challenge 2: Client-Side Audio Preview Latency
- **Constraint:** Instant feedback is crucial when tweaking sliders; waiting seconds for audio buffer decoding breaks the tactile illusion.
- **Selected Approach:** Leveraged Web Audio API `AudioContext` with pre-cached audio preview buffers, allowing immediate crossfading between candidate matches.
